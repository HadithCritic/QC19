under the Simplified29 text mode. Generated by comparing the engine's
segmentations of `data/content.db` (classic, Tanzil) and `data/submission.db`
(Submission, wikisubmission.org). The classic column leaves out the Bismillah
the classic text prefixes to verse 1; the Submission edition stores it as verse 0.

Structural differences, not listed row by row:

- Chapter 9 ends at verse 127; 9:128 and 9:129 are not part of the edition.
- Chapters 2 to 114, except 9, have their Bismillah as verse 0 (112 verses),
  which can be left uncounted. Chapter 1's Bismillah is verse 1.
